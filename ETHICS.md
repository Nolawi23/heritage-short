# Ethics, consent and licences

## Consent record

| Person (role) | What is used | Consent given by | Date | Scope |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mom (her call in shot 1) | her own words ("Nolawi! Delina! Come down here!"), spoken by a preset voice, not her voice | Mom | recorded 2026-11-25 | this film, family chat and course submission |
| The family (author, parents, siblings) | memories in `story/notes.md`; shown only from behind, as silhouettes or as hands | the author, for the family | recorded 2026-11-25 | this film, family chat and course submission |

No real photographs, recordings or voices of family members are used. Characters are **described** in text and
generated by FLUX; voices are **preset Gemini TTS voices** (`narration.voices[*].voice`). Voice cloning is never used:
Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS also offers voice design, replication and remixing, and the pipeline calls none of them
(`pipeline/narration.py` only sends a preset voice name). TTS requests are sent with `store=False`.

## Labelling plan

- Every export ends with an end card drawn in code that always contains **"Made with AI"** (`pipeline/assemble.py`,
  `end_card()`), whatever `end_card.text` says.
- Descriptions and posts that share the film say that images, motion, voices and music are AI-generated, and name the models.
- `runs.jsonl` is committed so every generated item can be traced to its model, prompt and seed.

## Privacy: keep private details out of free endpoints

- **Gemini API free tier:** Google's pricing page lists "content used to improve our products: Yes" for the free tier.
  `story/notes.md`, narration text and the script prompt go there, so they must not contain full names, addresses,
  dates of birth, health or financial details. `pipeline/script.py` and `story/notes.md` repeat this warning.
- **Cloudflare Workers AI:** image prompts and the approved reference sheet are sent to Workers AI.
- **Hugging Face Space (music fallback):** only the music caption and metadata (bpm, key, duration, seed) are sent;
  no names or family details.
- **Kaggle:** keyframes are uploaded as a **private** dataset and the notebooks are **private** kernels. The dataset
  has to declare a licence; it is `CC0-1.0` (Kaggle rejected `copyright-authors`), which is acceptable only because it
  holds nothing but AI-generated keyframes and stays private.
- Secrets live only in `.env` (gitignored); `make check-secrets` and the pre-commit hook block keys in tracked files, and
  the Kaggle username is redacted from logs.
