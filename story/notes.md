# Story notes (PLACEHOLDER)

Write the story here, then run `make script` to draft `storyboard.json` from it
(`make script FORCE=1` to overwrite the current storyboard; otherwise the draft goes to `storyboard.draft.json`).

**Privacy:** this file is sent to the Gemini API. On the free tier Google may use prompts to improve its
products, so keep private family details out of it: no full names, addresses, dates of birth, health or
financial details. Use first names or roles ("Mom", "Grandfather") and the memories themselves.

Suggested outline:
- Who the film is for (audience) and what they should feel (intent)
- The memory or tradition at the centre, and where it happens
- Shot 1 opens with MOM calling out (one short line), then the NARRATOR
- 3-5 concrete sensory details (objects, sounds, light, food)
- How it ends
- Voices: who is NARRATOR (preset Gemini voice, never a cloned voice), who is MOM
